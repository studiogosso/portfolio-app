About

Profile

Shota Goshozona is a creator working across Japan and France as an animator, episode director, screenwriter, and director.

In Japan, he contributed key animation and direction at studios including MAPPA, working on major titles such as "Jujutsu Kaisen," "Chainsaw Man," and "Mob Psycho 100."

Now based in France, he directs 3D CG animation projects, pursuing a new visual language that fuses Japanese and French sensibilities.
